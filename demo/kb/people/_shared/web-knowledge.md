## SG

- MOM WSH 总入口：https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health
- MOM 新闻稿（2022-09-01）：建筑等行业实施 weekly coordination meetings 与 daily toolbox briefings。https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2022/0109-heightened-safety-period-measures-to-address-spate-of-workplace-fatalities
- WSHC 页题 *Guide to Effective Toolbox Meeting*（2018-04-17；Construction and Landscape）：每天开工前或换班前；六步：适工、PPE、当日任务与危险、Look Think Do、报告不安全、收工整理。发现危险立刻停并上报。toolbox ≠ 安全技术交底。https://www.tal.sg/wshc/resources/publications/guides-and-handbooks/guide-to-effective-toolbox-meeting
- 指南 PDF：https://www.tal.sg/wshc/-/media/tal/wshc/resources/publications/guides-and-handbooks/files/toolbox_meeting_guide.pdf
- MOM CSMS 数据指引（2018-10-24）：分包须在工作开始前做 daily Toolbox Meeting；记录主持、出席、议题、身心状况。https://www.mom.gov.sg/-/media/mom/documents/safety-health/csms-system-integration-data-guidelines.pdf
- BCA 页题 *Construction site records*（页更 2026-01-29）：现场保管 site record book 等；QP 负责；site supervisor 记在场时间与意见/指令。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/permit-for-structural-works/construction-site-records/
- WSH Act 各方责任（雇主提供 instruction / training / supervision；工人遵守 SWP）：https://www.mom.gov.sg/workplace-safety-and-health/workplace-safety-and-health-act/responsibilities-of-stakeholders
