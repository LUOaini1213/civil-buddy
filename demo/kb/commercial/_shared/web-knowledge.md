# 商务造价大类 · 联网入口（2026-08-14 现场）

本大类五岗共用：造价、变更签证、索赔调概、分包结算、验工计价。条目标辖区。未抽出原文的标准只写全名，条款 UNSPECIFIED。本页不是规范全文库。公司层总入口见 `company/web-portals.md`。禁止编综合单价和「市场价约 xx」。

## SG

- BCA PSSCOC 总入口（页更 **2026-03-14**）：https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/procurement/standard-contract-forms/public-sector-standard-conditions-of-contract-psscoc/
- 现用主文本：**PSSCOC for Construction Works 2020（8th edition Jul 2020）**；另有 **PSSCOC for Design & Build 2020（7th edition Jul 2020）**、**PSSCOC-lite for Construction Works 2025**、**PSSCOC-lite for Design & Build 2025**、**Standard Conditions for Nominated Sub-Contract 2008（5th edition December 2008）**。
- 官方 PDF 目录**只写条款名**（本轮打开）：Clause 19 Variations to the Works；Clause 20 Valuation of Variations；Clause 21 Measurement；Clause 22 Claims for Loss and Expense；Clause 23 Procedure for Claims；Clause 30 Assignment and Subcontracting；Clause 32 Progress Payments and Final Account。天数未抽或未点名用户文本则 UNSPECIFIED。
- SIA 售卖（未抽出条文）：**SIA Building Contract 2016**（With Quantities / Without Quantities / Design & Build，Domestic & International）、**SIA Sub-Contract 2016**。https://sia.org.sg/publications/
- REDAS 售卖（未抽出条文）：**REDAS Design & Build Conditions of Main Contract 4th Edition**；**REDAS Design & Build Conditions of Sub-Contract 2nd Edition**。https://redas.com/resources/industry-guides/
- SOPA：**Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 2004**（SSO 现行本 as at 14 Aug 2026）。条题：s.5 Entitlement to progress payments；s.9 Effect of "pay when paid provisions"；s.10 Payment claims；s.11 Payment responses, etc.；s.12 Entitlement to make adjudication applications。**时限 UNSPECIFIED**（本轮只抽到条题，未抽 s.10/s.11 正文天数）。https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/BCISPA2004
- BCA 解说（页更 **2026-08-05**）：书面合同且已按合同施工或供货者可发 payment claim；对方须作 payment response；不服可向 ANB（现为 Singapore Mediation Centre）申请 adjudication。主包、分包、供应商、顾问均受益。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/procurement/procurement-and-legal-frameworks/security-of-payment-act/
- IRAS Construction：letter of claim **不是** tax invoice。GST 现行标准税率见 IRAS 页述 **9%**，本岗不算税负。https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/specific-business-sectors/construction
- FIDIC 只写条款名（fidic.org 公开页未抽出天数，禁止编 28 天）：Clause 13 Variations and Adjustments；1999 Sub-Clause 20.1 Contractor's Claims / Clause 20 Claims, Disputes and Arbitration；2017 另列 Clause 21 Disputes。https://fidic.org/

## 通用提醒

- SG 商务先走用户合同（PSSCOC / SIA / REDAS）+ SOPA，不要把 GB 50500 清单组价当新加坡 BOQ。
- CN 项目才用清单计价标准与示范文本全名；DUAL 必须分栏。
- 无已标价清单 / 定额 / 询价：单价 TBD。SIA / REDAS / FIDIC 时限未抽原文则只写条款名。不写「报审通过」「可以开工」。
