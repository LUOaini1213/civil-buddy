# 计划大类 · 联网入口（2026-08-14 现场）

计划大类共享：总控、四周滚动、人机料负荷的公开门户。条目标辖区。未抽出原文的标准只写全名，条款 UNSPECIFIED。本页不是规范全文库，不编持续天数、工日或 C-Score 最低分。公司层总入口见 `company/web-portals.md`。

## SG

- BCA Buildability / Constructability（页更 **2026-07-27**）：GFA ≥ 5,000 m² 须满足最低 **B-Score** 与 **C-Score**；C-Score 衡量系统模板、爬升脚手架等省工工艺，**不是劳动力需用计划**。最低分见当期 Code of Practice on Buildability，本岗不默写分数。表格 CS01 / CCS01 只作门户。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/productivity/buildability-and-constructability-framework/
- Building Control (Buildability and Productivity) Regulations 2011（SSO 现行本 as at **14 Aug 2026**；最近修订 S 547/2026，2026-08-05）：目录名 **reg.5 Minimum constructability score**；**reg.8 Submission of constructability score**；**reg.9 Site records of construction techniques and processes**；**reg.10 Collection and submission of Construction Productivity Data**（定义含 manpower utilisation / construction output）。**reg.3A** 列 IDD 用例，含 digital scheduling。https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/BCA1989-S199-2011
- Integrated Buildability and Constructability Framework（B+C）为自愿试点（页述 GFA 5,000 m² 可报名），不是现行强制最低分。
- BCA PSSCOC（页更 **2026-03-14**）：现行主文本 **PSSCOC for Construction Works 2020（8th edition Jul 2020）**。进度计划条款**只写目录名**：**Clause 9 Programme for the Works**（9.1 Programme to be Furnished；9.2 Revision of Programme；9.3 Acceptance of Programme；9.4 Failure to Submit Adequate Programme）；工期 **Clause 14 Time for Completion**（14.2 Extension of the Time for Completion；14.3 Notice）。提交与修订以用户合同文本为准。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/procurement/standard-contract-forms/public-sector-standard-conditions-of-contract-psscoc/
- Lean Construction Institute · **Last Planner System®**：只作方法名。五句对话 should / can / will / did / learn。不是 BCA / PSSCOC 法定档。https://leanconstruction.org/lean-topics/last-planner-system/
