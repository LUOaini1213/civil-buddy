## SG

- CORENET X 总入口（页标 14 Aug 2026）：https://info.corenet.gov.sg/ 。四项变化：新报审流程（RABW）、IFC+SG 格式 BIM、提交门户、Code of Practice。首页仍写：自 2025-10-01 起 GFA≥30,000 m² 新项目须经本门户报审。
- COP 现行本（本轮打开仍挂现行）：CORENET X Code of Practice **3.1 Edition（2025-12）**。用途：跨网关多机构报审准备；含多专业坐标 / geo-referencing。**不替代**各局 Handbook / Circular / Act。https://info.corenet.gov.sg/regulatory-process/corenet-x-code-of-practice
- COP 页述定义：RABW = Regulatory Approval Process for Building Works；**Federated Model** = 多专业合成模型，平面 **SVY21（EPSG:3414）**、高程 **SHD**；**Level of Details** = 只要嵌了 IFC+SG 数据与最小尺寸即可（树可用棒棒糖体）。禁止写成 AIA/BIM Forum LOD 强制档。
- RABW 默认三网关：Design / Construction / Completion；**Piling 可选**；简单类型可走 **DSP**。另有 External Works、Independent Submissions。https://info.corenet.gov.sg/regulatory-process/by-key-gateways
- IFC+SG = IFC 开放标准 + 新加坡属性（**SGPset**），供 CORENET X 审阅；不是「裸 IFC4」。入门页述 IFC4 + SGPsets。https://info.corenet.gov.sg/ifc-sg/start-here/WhatIsIFCSG
- 旧称 BIM e-submission：2016 起 BCA/URA 已收 BIM（尤其 GFA>5,000 sqm），模型须正确地理定位。https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/best-practices/geo-referencing-bim-submissions/
- 强制节奏以较新联合通告为准。DC25-07 表中「2026-10-01 全部新项目不论 GFA」已被 APPBCA-2026-12 / URA/PB/2026/08-DCG（2026-07-23）收窄：2026-10-01 起仅新项目 GFA≥5,000 m² 强制走 Gateway Processes；GFA<5,000 m² 可继续 CORENET 2.0。https://info.corenet.gov.sg/docs/default-source/bca-circulars/circular-for-updates-to-corenet-x-implementation-plan.pdf?sfvrsn=d8a19259_1
- BCA Building Plan submission（页更 **2026-07-06**）：截至该页，≥30,000 m² 新项目走 CORENET X，其余仍可 CORENET 2.0。不得把该页写成「已全部强制」。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/safety-and-standards/applications-and-licenses/building-plan-submission/
- 质量模型资源（页更 **2026-08-07**）：**Model Content Requirements V2.0 Mar 2026**、BIM Handover Technical Guide。无结构化信息则协调失效、返工。https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/productivity/idd-integrated-digital-delivery/ensuring-quality-bim-models/

## 国际名称（CN/SG 均可点，勿当地方强制档）

- ISO 19650 总题：Organization and digitization of information about buildings and civil engineering works, including building information modelling (BIM) — Information management using building information modelling。分册：-1 Concepts and principles（2018）；-2 Delivery phase of the assets（2018）；-3 Operational phase of the assets（2020）；-4 Information exchange（2022）；-5 Security-minded approach to information management（2020，https://www.iso.org/standard/74206.html）；-6 Health and safety information（2025）。https://www.iso.org/sectors/building-construction/building-information-modelling
- IFC：ISO 16739-1:2024 Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) for data sharing in the construction and facility management industries — Part 1: Data schema。页述含 property and quantity set definitions。https://www.iso.org/standard/84123.html
- BIM Forum **2025 LOD Specification**（2025-12-31 发布）：自述不规定某阶段必须到哪一档；矩阵待填，禁止写「必须 LOD 300/400」。https://bimforum.org/resource/lod-level-of-development-lod-specification/
